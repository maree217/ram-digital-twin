CASE STUDY 1: Housing Association Digital Transformation
CLIENT: Large Housing Association (50,000+ properties)
CHALLENGE: Legacy systems, manual processes, poor tenant satisfaction
DURATION: 18 months
BUDGET: £2.5M

SITUATION:
- Multiple disconnected legacy systems
- Manual paper-based processes for 70% of operations
- Tenant satisfaction at 65% (industry target: 85%)
- Average response time to tenant requests: 10 days
- High staff turnover due to inefficient processes

INTERVENTION:
1. Comprehensive process mapping and system audit
2. Dynamics 365 implementation with custom housing modules
3. Power Platform automation for key workflows
4. Staff training and change management program
5. Phased rollout across 12 regional offices

RESULTS:
- 60% reduction in manual processing time
- Tenant response time improved to 2 days average
- Tenant satisfaction increased to 88%
- Staff productivity increased by 45%
- Annual operational savings of £800k
- ROI achieved within 15 months

KEY SUCCESS FACTORS:
- Strong executive sponsorship and communication
- Phased implementation reducing risk
- Comprehensive staff training and support
- Focus on user experience and adoption
